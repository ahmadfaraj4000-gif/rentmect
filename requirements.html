Before You Book

Rental Requirements

Here is what customers need before renting from Rent Me CT.

Driver Requirements

  • Must be at least 21 years old.
  • Renters under 25 may be subject to a young driver fee.
  • Must have a valid, unexpired driver’s license.
  • Only approved drivers listed on the rental may operate the vehicle.

Documents Needed

  • Driver’s license photo upload.
  • Proof of active auto insurance.
  • Billing/contact information.
  • Signed rental agreement and addendum.

Payments & Deposit

  • Rental payment is due before pickup.
  • Sales tax and applicable fees are collected at checkout.
  • Security deposit is required and varies by vehicle class.
  • Post-rental charges may apply for damage, tolls, tickets, cleaning, smoking, mileage, or late return.

Mileage & Return

  • 200 miles per day included.
  • Extra mileage billed at $0.35 per mile.
  • Vehicle must be returned by the scheduled date and time.
  • Late returns may result in additional charges.

Vehicle Care

  • No smoking in any vehicle.
  • No racing, towing, delivery, rideshare, or illegal use.
  • Customer is responsible for damage during the rental period.
  • Pickup and return photos may be required.

Pickup Location

  • Rent Me CT
  • 12 Holmes Circle
  • Farmington, CT
  • Pickup and return times available between 9 AM and 9 PM.
