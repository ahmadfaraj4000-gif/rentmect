Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

Last updated July 30, 2026. This page lists the cookies and browser-storage categories used by the public site and client portal.

Current Use at a Glance

The reviewed Rent Me CT public website does not create advertising cookies with document.cookie and does not currently load Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Plausible, PostHog, Meta Pixel, Google Ads, or TikTok Pixel. We use necessary local storage for booking handoff, portal authentication, security, and your privacy choice. Functional storage for display preferences is optional.

Necessary

Necessary storage supports a booking you requested, account authentication, security, fraud prevention, Stripe return handling, and preservation of your privacy choice. It cannot be disabled through our preference panel because the requested booking and portal would not operate reliably without it. You can still clear it in browser settings, which may sign you out or erase an unfinished booking selection.

Functional

Functional storage remembers optional display preferences, such as whether an on-site promotion was dismissed. Rejecting functional storage does not prevent a rental, but a preference may not be remembered between visits.

Analytics

No analytics provider is currently active on the reviewed public site or client portal. If we add one, it must remain disabled until analytics consent is available where required, and this inventory will be updated with its name, purpose, owner, and duration.

Marketing

No advertising pixel or cross-site behavioral advertising cookie is currently active on the reviewed public site or client portal. Email-marketing and recurring transactional-SMS choices are separate from cookie choices and remain governed by their own unchecked consent controls.

Third-Party Services

Stripe, Supabase, Google Maps or Places, Wheelbase/Outdoorsy, GitHub Pages, and other providers may use cookies, local storage, or service logs when their features, hosted pages, or links are used. These are controlled by the provider. Review its policy and browser controls. Opening a third-party insurance or map link may take you to a separate website with its own choices.

Manage or Disable Storage

The privacy banner offers Accept all, Reject non-essential, and Customize. Your category choices, policy version, and timestamp are saved so the site can honor them. Use at any time to review or change those choices. You can also delete cookies, local storage, and cached data in browser settings or use private-browsing controls. Blocking all storage may prevent sign-in, booking handoff, document upload, or secure checkout return. A recognized Global Privacy Control signal keeps analytics and marketing categories off.
