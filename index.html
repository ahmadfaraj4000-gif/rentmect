Hartford Car Rental FAQs

Renting a car near Hartford, CT

Does Rent Me CT serve Hartford, Connecticut?

Yes. Rent Me CT serves Hartford, West Hartford, New Britain, Farmington, Bradley International Airport travelers, and surrounding Connecticut communities. Vehicle pickup is at 12 Holmes Circle in Farmington unless another arrangement is confirmed.

What types of vehicles can I rent from Rent Me CT?

Rent Me CT offers sedans, SUVs, luxury vehicles, trucks, and passenger vans depending on current fleet availability.

Can I book a one-day rental?

Yes. Rental length depends on current vehicle availability.

How many miles are included?

Rentals include 250 miles per day. Extra mileage is charged at $0.35 per mile unless another mileage option is purchased or agreed to.

Do I need insurance to rent?

Renters must provide valid auto insurance unless Rent Me CT confirms otherwise in writing. Upload the insurance declaration page showing the policyholder, active dates, and coverage; other insurance documents may be rejected.

How should I enter my name for identity verification?

Enter your full legal name exactly as it appears on your government ID. If the ID shows a middle name, type it immediately after your first name so Stripe Identity can match it.
