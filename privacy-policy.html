Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 26, 2026. This policy explains what information Rent Me CT collects through this website and related rental systems.

Information We Collect

On the public website, customers may enter pickup dates, return dates, pickup times, return times, and vehicle choices to start a reservation. If you continue into checkout or the customer portal, Rent Me CT or its service providers may collect your name, email, phone number, address, driver license information, insurance proof, rental agreement details, payment status, rental dates, vehicle selection, and messages about your rental.

Local Storage, Cookies & Cache

The public website stores pending booking details in your browser's local storage so your selected dates, times, and vehicle can carry into the booking flow. The reviewed frontend does not set its own first-party advertising cookies with document.cookie. Your browser may still cache pages, images, scripts, and API responses, and third-party services may use their own cookies, storage, or logs under their own policies.

How We Use Information

We use information to respond to inquiries, process reservations, verify eligibility, review license and insurance documents, manage payments and deposits, communicate pickup and return details, send rental reminders when enabled, prevent fraud, protect vehicles, enforce rental agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

SMS Communications & Consent

When you request a phone-verification code, Rent Me CT uses the mobile number you provide to send the requested one-time security message. Separately, you may choose through an unchecked consent box to receive recurring transactional text messages concerning bookings, payments, documents, pickup, returns, extensions, and customer support. This recurring SMS choice is optional and is not a condition of purchasing or renting a vehicle.

Message frequency varies according to your rental activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe from recurring messages or HELP for assistance. We may retain the phone number, consent status, consent timestamp, consent source, disclosure version, and opt-out status to operate the program and document communication preferences.

Rent Me CT does not sell or share mobile phone information or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. We may provide this information to service providers, including Twilio, only as necessary to transmit messages, maintain consent and suppression records, secure the service, or comply with law. Those providers may not use Rent Me CT SMS consent information for their own marketing.

See the Rent Me CT SMS Program Terms for program details.

Third-Party Services

This site and related rental systems may use Wheelbase/Outdoorsy for reservation checkout and availability, Supabase for customer portal data and document storage, Stripe for payments and identity verification, Twilio for SMS reminders or phone verification, Google Maps for address links, and listed insurance-provider websites for optional insurance research. When you leave Rent Me CT or use a third-party checkout, that provider's terms and privacy policy apply.

Stripe Identity Verification

Rent Me CT uses Stripe Identity to help confirm that a renter is the person shown on a government-issued identity document. Stripe may collect identity-document images, facial or selfie images, information extracted from the document such as an ID number and address, device information, advanced fraud signals, and biometric identifiers used to compare the selfie with the document photo. Stripe shares verification results with Rent Me CT and uses information to operate, secure, prevent fraud in, and improve its services according to the choices and consent presented in Stripe's verification flow.

Rent Me CT's customer portal stores the Stripe VerificationSession reference, status, mode, error code, and verification timestamps; it does not copy the raw Stripe ID or selfie images into the Rent Me CT database. Authorized Rent Me CT personnel may have access to verification information through Stripe under Stripe's controls. Learn more in Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Contact Rent Me CT if you need to request deletion or redaction of verification data, revoke consent, or ask whether an alternative verification method is available. Stripe may also require a separate request for information it controls or retains under applicable law.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to operate rentals, process payments, verify documents, send communications, provide customer support, comply with law, enforce agreements, protect our vehicles, and work with service providers. Rent Me CT does not sell personal information through the reviewed public website.

Your Choices

You can clear this website's local storage in your browser settings. You can change the optional recurring SMS preference in the customer portal or reply STOP to a Rent Me CT text. You can contact Rent Me CT to request help with account information, rental records, or communication preferences. Some rental records must be retained for legal, tax, fraud prevention, safety, insurance, agreement-enforcement, and consent-recordkeeping reasons.
